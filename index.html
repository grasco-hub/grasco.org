About GRASCO

Grassroots Resource and Skills for Community Opportunities (GRASCO) is a community-focused organization working to strengthen organized communities and self-help groups through practical skills, leadership, systems and opportunities.

Our Mission

To strengthen grassroots communities and self-help groups with the knowledge, leadership, systems, skills and opportunities needed to build sustainable livelihoods and stronger communities.

Our Vision

Strong, organized and self-sustaining communities where people work together, create opportunities and build lasting community wealth.

Our Approach

GRASCO focuses on practical support, capacity building, accountability, enterprise development, market linkages and partnerships that help community initiatives become more sustainable.

The Challenge We Respond To

Kilifi County has a vibrant community spirit and many organized groups. GRASCO responds to common organizational and economic challenges that can prevent promising groups from reaching their full potential.

Leadership & Governance

Weak leadership, governance and accountability can affect decision-making and group performance.

Financial Management

Limited knowledge of financial management, records and transparent resource use can weaken sustainability.

Unity & Participation

Internal conflicts, mistrust and declining participation can weaken collective action.

Enterprise Skills

Limited business planning and enterprise management can restrict profitability and growth.

Markets & Opportunities

Groups can face difficulty accessing markets, financial services and development opportunities.

Project & Proposal Skills

Limited capacity to prepare proposals, manage projects and engage partners can reduce opportunities.

Mentorship

Emerging community leaders need continuous coaching, practical guidance and support.

Networking

Weak connections between groups can reduce opportunities for learning, collaboration and markets.

GRASCO's approach: These challenges are not a judgement on communities. They point to the need for continuous capacity building, practical leadership development and organizational strengthening.

Membership

GRASCO membership provides an organized pathway for people and community groups to connect with capacity building, information, opportunities, partnerships and practical support.

1. Registration Form

Prospective members complete the official GRASCO registration process and provide the information required for membership records and verification.

Request Registration

2. Benefits

Members can access relevant training, mentoring, community information, networking opportunities, market and partnership linkages, events and other GRASCO services made available to members.

3. Member Responsibilities

Members are expected to uphold GRASCO's values, provide accurate information, participate responsibly in programmes and activities, respect the organization's policies and leadership structures, protect confidential information, meet applicable obligations and contribute positively to the development of their communities.

4. Why Join GRASCO?

Join a structured community network that promotes stronger leadership, better organization, practical skills, enterprise development, accountability, networking and access to opportunities.

Membership principle: GRASCO exists to strengthen organized communities. Self-help groups remain represented by their own leadership while accessing GRASCO support and services.

GRASCO Community Group Support

GRASCO does not offer training alone. We first listen to the group’s challenges and identify the most appropriate practical support. Depending on the situation, GRASCO may provide guidance and counselling, advisory services, mediation and conflict resolution, marketing linkages, group registration guidance, leadership and governance support, enterprise development, training and capacity building, partnerships, and other relevant services.

What happens after you request support?

  1. GRASCO reviews your group's identified challenges.
  2. A preliminary discussion is conducted to understand the group's training needs.
  3. The appropriate support or combination of services is proposed to the group.
  4. The group confirms the support required and the applicable service arrangements.
