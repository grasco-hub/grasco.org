Introduction to GRASCO

GRASCO — Grassroots Resource and Skills for Community Opportunities — is a community-focused organization established to strengthen grassroots institutions and community self-help groups.

GRASCO focuses on practical capacity building, leadership and governance development, financial discipline, mentoring, market linkages, networking and sustainable community enterprise development.

Our approach is to strengthen what communities already have, support existing initiatives and help organized communities improve their ability to manage resources, develop enterprises, access markets and create sustainable opportunities.

Our Vision

Strong, organized and self-sustaining communities with empowered grassroots institutions.

Our Mission

To strengthen community self-help groups and grassroots leadership through capacity building, governance, financial discipline, mentorship, market linkages and sustainable development support.

Our Approach

Practical, participatory, needs-based and focused on strengthening existing community initiatives.

GRASCO Self-Help Group Development Standard

The GRASCO-GDS provides a structured framework for strengthening community self-help groups and their ability to operate effectively, transparently and sustainably.

1. Identity

Clear group identity, purpose and direction.

2. Leadership

Responsible and effective leadership.

3. Membership

Organized and active membership.

4. Governance

Clear rules, responsibilities and accountability.

5. Financial Management

Proper records and responsible financial practices.

6. Records

Reliable organizational and operational records.

7. Planning

Practical planning and measurable activities.

8. Enterprise

Strengthening viable community economic activities.

9. Market Access

Improving connections to markets and opportunities.

10. Skills

Continuous development of practical skills.

11. Networking

Building productive institutional relationships.

12. Sustainability

Building systems that can continue over time.

GRASCO Organizational Structure

GRASCO connects its leadership and grassroots coordination structures with the administrative levels through which communities are organized.

County Level

Executive leadership provides overall organizational direction, coordination and accountability.

Sub-County Level

Chapter management coordinates GRASCO activities and implementation within the Sub-County.

Ward Level

Cluster coordination supports grassroots engagement, communication and implementation at Ward level.

Village / Hamlet Level

Grassroots representatives maintain direct links with communities and participating groups.
